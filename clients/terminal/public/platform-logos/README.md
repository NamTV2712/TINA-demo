# Meeting platform icons

These bundled icons are shown only to identify the provider of a meeting record in TINA Terminal.

- `google-meet.svg` — Google Workspace Help product icon, downloaded from `https://knowledge.workspace.google.com/static/images/icons/product/meet.svg`.
- `zoom.png` — PNG conversion of the Zoom site favicon at `https://www.zoom.com/favicon.ico`.
- `microsoft-teams.png` — PNG conversion of the Microsoft Teams site favicon at `https://teams.microsoft.com/favicon.ico`.
- `discord.svg` — Discord symbol from Discord's official brand kit. It is displayed in Discord Blurple, the supplied white-symbol colorway.

Assets were collected on 2026-10-30. The original marks belong to their respective owners; TINA does not imply affiliation or endorsement.
